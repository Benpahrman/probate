## Platform Core Formula & Doctrine Summary

### 1. The Core Triad
$$\text{Ownership} \neq \text{Control} \neq \text{Authority}$$
- **Ownership:** Who holds recorded legal title.
- **Control:** Who holds physical keys and family consensus.
- **Authority:** Who holds legal statutory capacity under probate statutes to execute the deed.

### 2. Opportunity Scoring Formula
$$\text{Gross Upside} = (\text{Equity} \times 0.35) + (\text{Authority} \times 0.30) + (\text{Distress} \times 0.20) + (\text{Liquidity} \times 0.15)$$
$$\text{Composite Score (0–100)} = \text{Gross Upside} - \text{Deal Friction Score (DFS)}$$
- **Priority A (Flash):** 85–100 points (4-hour SLA push)
- **Priority B (Weekly Batch):** 65–84 points (Tuesday batch drop)
- **Priority C (Monitor):** 50–64 points (automated docket watch)
- **Disqualified:** $<50$ points (quarantined)

### 3. The 6-Gate Pass
All certified opportunities must score $\ge 70$ PAS, possess clean title vesting, $\ge \$50\text{k}$ net equity, reachable decision-makers, actionable statutory authority (RCW Title 11), and composite score $\ge 50$.
